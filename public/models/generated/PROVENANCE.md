# Sola reconstruction

`sola-hunyuan.glb` was generated on 24 October 2026 using fal's Hunyuan3D v3.1 Pro image-to-3D model with PBR enabled. The input was an AI-generated material study derived from the user's Sola / Verte Figma illustration, not a photograph of a manufactured product. `sola-source.png` is the crop of that original Figma artwork.

The raw output was 53,788,048 bytes. Offline optimization used glTF Transform and meshoptimizer with a 0.0005 relative error bound, retained 149,990 triangles, clamped invalid specular factors to the glTF range, and encoded three 2048×2048 PBR maps as WebP. The web GLB is 6,692,040 bytes. No extra paid service was used for this optimization.

In Kalpi the model is normalized to the design's proposed 64 × 38 × 118 mm dimensions, explicitly unconfirmed. Original Figma label artwork is applied as a separate editable layer in the comparison draft. Raw generated lettering, rear artwork and unseen geometry are inferred. This is a textured reconstruction, not a manufacturing or measured optical model.

One initial TRELLIS.2 attempt was rejected for poor textures. Two paid submissions total: listed estimates $0.35 + $0.525 = $0.875, below the user's $1 cap. Actual billing was not independently verified. Paid retries are not allowed under this task's remaining budget.
