Universal pick one background for any product

The four shortlisted backgrounds were tested with the same model in 8 product colours: cream, jet black, gold, deep purple, teal, red, navy and silver. The pick is near-black with a soft velvet-plum glow from the top and a hint of petrol in the floor reflection. It is now the default on this page.

  • VerdictThe universal blend is the most even all-rounder. It matches plain near-black on separation, colour truth and ring contrast, and adds the plum warmth that makes it feel premium.
  • Why not the othersGlacier and Deep petrol lift the background towards the product, so dark products blend in sooner. Velvet plum on its own is a little lower on every product.
  • Still weakNo background gets a jet-black product to 3:1 at the edge (about 2.3:1 at best). It stays visible, but dark products would need a brighter rim light on that product.

Inter Tight 600 for headings, capped at 28px. Inter 400/500 for everything else, 13–16px. A heading is never bigger than the product.

Spacing and density

Very airy. The first screen holds the stage, one caption line and one input. The tabs shrink to a small switch.

Pictures

The photo stands whole on a white mount, never cropped. Then the 3D product sits on a glossy floor with a soft reflection and a contact shadow.

Buttons

One white pill, "Start with this idea", inside the input. Everything else is a text link or a quiet ghost chip, such as Replay.

Empty and loading

  • What changesThe first screen becomes a lit stage with her real product as the hero. The big headings go, and so does the tool frame. One input with one button sits under the stage.
  • Why it helps a sellerIn a few seconds she sees her photo become a product she can turn around, and then become versions to choose from. That moment is the whole promise of Kalpi.
  • Trade-offIt is the most work to build, and it needs a 3D model, which costs a little per product. Older phones get a still picture instead. The dark stage also has to match the rest of the site.

Fixes for any option

Small things I saw on the live page. Each one is worth doing whichever direction you pick.

  1. Some pictures can still be cropped. The default rule fills the frame and cuts the edges. Only the source pictures are switched to "contain". Use object-fit: contain everywhere a product is shown.
    kalpi-vote.html · .pic > img (line 125)
  2. Lavender bands under square pictures in the Gallery. Square photos, like the phone cases, sit in a 4:5 frame with a lavender fill below them. Fill the frame with a neutral that matches the photo backdrop, or let each card take its picture's own shape.
    kalpi-vote.html · .pic.msrc { background: #e9e4f7 } and .gal .pic.msrc { aspect-ratio: 4/5 }
  3. Gallery cards don't line up. One-line titles make shorter cards, so the Save buttons sit at different heights in the same row. Pin the button to the bottom of the card (flex column, margin-top: auto).
    kalpi-vote.html · Gallery card markup in shelf(), grid .gal
  4. The status line is cut off with "…". "VOTES ARE A SIMULAT…" and "THE IMAGE SERVICE T…" are truncated in tiny uppercase mono. Use sentence case at 12px, and let the line wrap or shorten the words.
    kalpi-vote.html · .sfoot, .sfoot span (lines 67 and 272)
  5. The main button looks broken before you type. While disabled, it fades to 50% and keeps its orange dot, so it reads as an error. Keep it at full colour with a clear "Type an idea first" hint, or use a calm disabled style without the dot.
    kalpi-vote.html · .btn:disabled, .btn.pri .dot
  6. Empty dark space under the Studio. The frame is at least 720px tall, so the short start screen leaves a large dotted gap. Size the frame to its content, or make the start screen fill it.
    kalpi.html · .kx-frame iframe { height: max(720px, …) }
  7. The helper badge looks like debug output. The green mono pill is pushed 4px right of the sentence and wraps onto its own line. Use a small dot with plain words in the body font, aligned to the text.
    kalpi.html · .kx-state, .kx-state.on
  8. Mismatched tabs and stacked headings. The Studio tab is twice as wide as Gallery, and each tab has its own gradient. Then "What do you want to make?" repeats the section heading at the same large size. Use one small tab switch and one heading. Also hide the textarea's resize grip with resize: none.
    kalpi.html · .kx-tabs, .kx-h · kalpi-vote.html · .start h1, textarea.fld
