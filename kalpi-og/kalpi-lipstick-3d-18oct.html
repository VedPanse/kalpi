Who it's for

People with an idea and no savings to gamble: students, parents at home, anyone after a side income. Not for anyone looking to get rich quick.

Straight answers

Will this make me money?

Nobody can promise that, and you should walk away from anyone who does. Here's what you get: a real answer about whether people want it, before you pay for inventory.

Is it honest to advertise something that isn't made yet?

Yes, when the page says so. Every page and every ad says coming soon. A deposit is refundable, and it is charged only if the maker's minimum order is reached.

Does a vote mean a sale?

No. That's why the winner is judged on deposits, not votes.

People committed. Now what?

You take your design and your numbers to a maker. Makers have a minimum order, so you will buy inventory. The difference is that now you know people are waiting for it.

What does it cost?

The price of a test isn't set yet. You'll see the full cost before you start one, and we'll never call something free unless it is.

Is it live?

The Studio is, for any product. Asking your people, the waitlist and deposits are what we're building next.
