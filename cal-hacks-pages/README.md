# Cal Hacks 13 pages

Standalone three.js pages made during the event. Open any `.html` in a browser (serve the folder, e.g. `python3 -m http.server`).

- `bottle/bottle.html`: bottle maker (lathe bottle, caps, finishes, liquid, label, ml, A/B/C versions)
- `store-rooms/store-rooms.html`: four store room templates (Gallery, Apothecary, Stage, Pop-up Loft) with clickable, customisable products
- `shapes/shapes.html`: shape studio (10 shapes × 6 finishes)
- `torus/torus.html`: torus playground
- `build-log/build-log.html`: how the walk-in store was built, step by step
- `calhacks-field.html`: past Cal Hacks winners and the field
- `research-gaps.md`: research gaps behind Label to Shelf
